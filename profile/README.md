# Nyria

Nyria turns the trade alerts you already send into orders in your own brokerage account. Post in Discord or Telegram, fire a TradingView or TrendSpider alert, or call a webhook, and a bot places the order at Charles Schwab, Tradier, tastytrade or Alpaca. Paper trading on Nyria Paper is free, with no card.

Creators can also sell access to a strategy: members pay, and each alert trades in every member's own account.
